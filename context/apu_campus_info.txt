ASIA PACIFIC UNIVERSITY OF TECHNOLOGY & INNOVATION (APU) - CAMPUS DIRECTORY & INFORMATION
================================================================================

1. GENERAL INFORMATION & LOCATION
----------------------------------
Institution: Asia Pacific University of Technology & Innovation (APU)
Campus Location: Technology Park Malaysia (TPM), Bukit Jalil, 57000 Kuala Lumpur, Wilayah Persekutuan Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia.
Main Contact: +603 8996 1000 / info@apu.edu.my
Website: https://www.apu.edu.my
Operating Hours: Monday – Friday: 8:30 AM – 6:00 PM | Saturday: 9:00 AM – 1:00 PM | Closed on Sundays and Public Holidays.

2. CAMPUS FACILITIES & SPECIALIZED LABS
----------------------------------------
- Library & Learning Resource Centre (Block B, Levels 3 & 4):
  Offers physical collections, digital databases, quiet study zones, collaborative discussion pods, and private research carrels.
  Hours: Mon-Fri: 8:30 AM – 8:00 PM; Sat: 9:00 AM – 2:00 PM.
- Specialized Technology Labs:
  * Cybersecurity Centre of Excellence & Security Operations Centre (SOC)
  * Artificial Intelligence & Robotics Innovation Centre
  * Cisco Networking & Telecommunications Labs
  * Mac Computing Suites & Multi-Platform Development Labs
  * Financial Trading Hall (Bloomberg Terminal integration)
  * Design & Media Studios (Green Screen, 3D printing, prototyping suites)
- Auditoriums & Lecture Theatres: Multi-tiered auditoriums equipped with acoustic treatments, high-resolution projection systems, and lecture-capture recording.

3. KEY STUDENT SERVICES & CONTACT DIRECTORY
--------------------------------------------
- Student Services Centre (Block A, Level 4):
  Handles student inquiries, official letter requests, attendance issues, student ID card replacements, and general advice.
  Email: studentadmin@apu.edu.my
- International Student Services (ISS) & Visa Office:
  Assists international students with EMGS, Student Pass renewals, i-Kad processing, and arrival clearance.
  Email: visa@apu.edu.my
- Bursary / Finance Office (Block A, Level 3):
  Manages tuition fee statements, payment receipts, installment schedules, PTPTN loan inquiries, and scholarship disbursements.
  Payment Portal: APSpace / APU Online Payment Gateway.
  Email: bursary@apu.edu.my
- Examinations & Academic Registry:
  Oversees exam schedules, transcript requests, grading appeals, and graduation certification.
  Email: examinations@apu.edu.my
- Career & Placement Centre:
  Assists students with internships, industry partnerships, resume clinics, and campus career fairs.
  Email: career@apu.edu.my
- Counselling & Mental Wellness Unit:
  Provides confidential counseling, academic stress management, and mental wellbeing support.
  Email: counselling@apu.edu.my

4. CAMPUS AMENITIES, FOOD & DINING
-----------------------------------
- Main Cafeteria & Food Court (Block E, Ground Floor & Mezzanine):
  Wide selection of multi-cuisine food stalls: Malay (Halal), Chinese, Indian, Western, Middle Eastern, and vegetarian meals.
- Cafes & Convenience:
  Convenience stores for snacks and daily necessities, on-campus specialty coffee cafes, and vending machines across all levels.
- Prayer Rooms (Surau):
  Dedicated male and female prayer facilities with ablution areas located on Block D, Level 2 and Block E, Level 3.
- Printing & Stationery Shops:
  High-speed photocopiers, binding services, and stationery supplies located near the main cafeteria and student lounge.

5. TRANSPORTATION & SHUTTLE BUS SERVICES
-----------------------------------------
- APU Free Shuttle Bus Service:
  Regular shuttle buses operate between the APU Campus and:
  1. LRT Bukit Jalil Station (Sri Petaling Line) - Approx. every 15-20 minutes during peak hours.
  2. Official APU Student Accommodations (Fortune Park, Endah Promenade).
  Students must tap their physical APU Student ID or show the APSpace digital bus pass when boarding.
- Public Transit:
  LRT Sri Petaling Line connects to Bandar Tasik Selatan (TBS Terminal for buses and ERL Airport Transit) and KL Sentral.
- Parking Facilities:
  Multi-level student parking available on campus. Parking access is activated via the APU Student ID card after registration and fee payment at the Bursary counter.

6. STUDENT ACCOMMODATION
-------------------------
- On-Campus Residence:
  Ensuite, single, and twin-sharing air-conditioned rooms with high-speed Wi-Fi, 24/7 security card access, laundry rooms, and communal kitchens.
- Satellite Off-Campus Housing:
  Managed apartments at Endah Promenade and Fortune Park with scheduled free shuttle bus transit to campus.
- Accommodation Office Contact: residence@apu.edu.my

7. SPORTS & RECREATION
-----------------------
- Indoor Gymnasium & Fitness Centre (Block E, Level 4): Free access for registered students with fitness equipment, free weights, and cardio machines.
- Outdoor Sports: Futsal court, basketball court, and outdoor jogging pathways.
- Student Recreation & E-Sports Arena: Billiards, table tennis, and dedicated gaming lounge for recreation and student clubs.
